## Accepted Behavior

The Ruin Boss owns four actual HP80/radius14 cover pillars at room-local (160,120), (480,120), (160,240) and (480,240). Each is a native StaticBody2D world obstacle and weapon Hurtbox. Pure BossArenaRuntime state owns hit points, geometry, stable identity and accepted damage claims. Pillars have no independent HealthComponent, counted enemy roster membership, Boss bar, death receipt or reward source. Central and perimeter routes retain at least48px clearance.

Sword, Bow, Gun, Staff and Gauntlets execute their existing native collision endpoints against each construct. Stable target identities derive from the owning hostile source and construct key, never a Node instance ID. Enemy reward/conversion endpoints remain enemy-only. Cover destruction retires both native collision layers; accepted claims prevent duplicate damage, while rejection restores the complete arena ledger, HP, actual collisions and Boss state.

An actual swept Boss charge collision destroys only its declared cover, retires that charge's damaging generation and supplies60 complete inclusive exposure frames. Frame rejection restores the pre-contact position, attack, cover and exposure; retry accepts the same contact once. Cover state stays in fixed room coordinates while the Boss moves.

The sealed Ruin beam checks actual declared cover between its committed origin and the Player at every offset. Intact cover blocks all three hits; destroyed cover opens the same path. This adjudication preserves the original committed geometry and damage identity, so no beam tracking or hidden new generation occurs. The construct projection also seals its own visibility, correct raster atlas/frame, tint, scale and offset; an invisible or incorrectly displayed obstacle rejects both frame preparation and weapon collision.

The actual `guardian_enrage_collapse` first active hit destroys every surviving declared cover only after the full75-frame warning. Damage facts spend each pillar's remaining HP, retain prior partial damage and skip already broken pillars. Rejection restores exact arena claims, original HP and both native collision layers; accepted retry destroys them once. Current cold reconstruction retains their permanent destruction without body damage, counted deaths or rewards. This adds no runtime field or content binding transition.

Only Ruin Boss runtime advances to schema2 with explicit `arena_state`. The other four Bosses preserve schema1. Exact historical Ruin schema1 snapshots initialize the authored intact arena at their retained frame; unknown historical fields, inconsistent HP/claims and malformed current state reject. BossActor, Driver and Host normalize only this declared historical runtime before comparing the complete reconstructed native aggregate. No other fields are omitted from that comparison.
