## Retained Behavior

- The actual Forest Boss owns six stable HP100/radius12 roots. Native tests use the authored Forest Boss room and its real Boss anchor. Placement preserves48px central and perimeter passages.
- Roots have independent permanent HP and authenticated Player weapon damage receipts. Overkill clamps to remaining root HP, duplicate damage rejects, and roots never join enemy/Boss groups or create separate rewards. The trunk remains directly hittable.
- A root break exposes the body for exactly45 accepted frames. A refused native frame restores HP, claims, exposure and collision; the same weapon identity retries successfully after compensation.
- Actual trunk damage crossing the60% threshold permanently retires the first three surviving roots in stable slot order. Broken roots remain broken. Phase refusal restores exact root state, trunk health and colliders; the retry applies damage once.
- Forest phase damage refreshes native root geometry after domain settlement. This closes the stale-collider rejection exposed by the initial P2 native test.
- Root exposure participates in the existing Character conversion endpoint. Tail frames remain pending throughout the root window, start afterwards and pause intact if another root breaks. Cold validation rejects an early active tail.
- Native geometry checks reject altered root position, collision or artwork before frame preparation and weapon settlement. Actual owner death disables both root collider layers and hides all roots.
