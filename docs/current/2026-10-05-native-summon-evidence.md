## Retained Behavior

All nine authored support definitions have executable projections and deterministic
species runtimes. Actual children use native Health, Hurtbox, action/control clocks,
room motion and the shared hostile frame participant. Their owned leases refuse
recursive summons and rewards. Eight simultaneous live or warning slots are
admitted; excess work retains frozen positions and waits for a complete new spawn
warning after a slot becomes safe and available. Rejected birth, death and exact
480-frame expiry restore native bodies, claims, control clocks and encounter work.

Caller and Boss retirement cancels child bodies, projectiles, zones and statuses
in the same accepted frame. Children whose authored contract survives principal
death retain their own lifetime. Timeline echo final death reserves the approved
35-frame warning followed by one 20 Time damage explosion with radius 32.

Elite Wraith and Spore final death reserves two stable, unrewarded children.
Natural self-consumption authenticates the prepared terminal mechanism before
Health settles, so self-detonation and direct lethal damage share the same final
split behavior. A rejected consumption restores the entire principal and child
reservation; exact-frame retry reconstructs the same identities and warnings.
The native Driver also authenticates genuine outside-frame final death receipts.

Canonical authored actions bind child type, count, lifetime and owner retirement.
Cold snapshots reject internally consistent child substitution, altered retirement
flags and Mirroring rows lacking their principal's authored reservation. Mirroring
uses a separate stable identity namespace; its affix execution is evidenced by the
separate Mirroring milestone.

## Persistence Contract

Native Effects schema 2 adds `summons`. Native Driver schema 2 adds
`summon_actors` outside the counted principal roster. Each child keeps the ordinary
cold envelope: `schema_version`, `definition_id`, `identity`, `actor`, `health`.
Source bindings resolve actual children and their attacks; pending work maps to
the authenticated principal. Explicit schema 1 migration adds empty summon state
and invents no historical children. Authenticated terminal corpse nodes do not
count as live principals during a physical checkpoint.

The physical SaveService matrix writes real native children and reconstructs a
fresh Main scene, both while the caller lives and after an ordinary principal has
died. Two physics-frame synchronization points precede exact continuation
comparison so reconstructed transforms reach Godot's physics server.
